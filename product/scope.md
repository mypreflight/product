# Scope

What MyPreflight covers and what it deliberately leaves out.

Last reviewed: 2026-10-20

## Rule of thumb

If it would be a commercial passenger or cargo operation in real life, it's in scope.

## In scope

- Airliner operations, passenger and cargo airlines.
- Business and private jets carrying passengers commercially.
- Real airlines only, current and historic (e.g. Pan Am, US Airways).
- Real airframes by registration, with real markings (e.g. SP-LRC, a LOT 787-9, or a KLM 747 landing at Maho Beach, Sint Maarten).
- MSFS 2020 and MSFS 2024.

## Out of scope

- Helicopters, general aviation, fighter and military jets. They don't need airline briefing materials and aren't in the aircraft list. Users can request additions through a ticket.
- Private PPL-style flights, such as taking a partner up or cost-sharing flights.
- Custom or fictional airlines. Pilots can pick a real airline and pretend.
- Real-world flight training or operational use.
- Users who don't use SimBrief (we point them to tutorials, nothing more).

## Later, only on demand

- **X-Plane**, if user demand justifies it.
- **Translations.** English is the aviation and add-on standard, and translations would slow shipping. The platform is i18n-capable; German (friends translate professionally) and Polish are the likely first languages.
