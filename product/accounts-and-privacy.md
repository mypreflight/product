# Accounts and privacy

Sign-up, visibility, deletion, bans, emails and moderation.

Last reviewed: 2026-10-20

## Sign-up

- Email and password, Google, or Discord. Discord sign-up offers to join the MyPreflight Discord (optional).
- Google and Discord can be linked to an account later.
- Maybe Facebook later; not needed at the start.
- No SimBrief or Navigraph login; it might need an official agreement.

## Flight visibility

Private, link-only or public. See `decisions.md`.

## What's public about a pilot

Name and surname, hours flown, types flown, stats. Profiles are private today; public profiles are planned.

## User-generated content

- Principle: the less user-generated content, the better.
- Some free-text fields and file uploads exist. User content is never republished.
- Logs are kept so we can help the authorities in case of illegal content.

## Deletion

- Self-serve account deletion.
- We keep only a hash of the deleted email, so that address can't sign up again (anti-abuse). This applies to everyone.
- A returning user can ask on Discord and gets unblocked without hesitation.

## Bans

By email hash and by IP. IP bans are expected to be rare.

## Emails

- Mandatory transactional: bans, password recovery and similar.
- Flight-progress updates: e.g. "you haven't finished your flight, something may be off".
- No newsletter yet. Preference switches and opt-ins exist.

## Legal pages

- Terms and conditions: mypreflight.io/terms-and-conditions
- Privacy policy: mypreflight.io/privacy-policy

They state exactly what data is collected. The live pages are the source of truth; the summary below is for orientation only.
