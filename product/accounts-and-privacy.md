# Accounts and privacy

Sign-up, visibility, deletion, bans, emails and moderation.

Last reviewed: 2026-10-20

## Sign-up

- Email and password, Google, or Discord. Discord sign-up offers to join the MyPreflight Discord (optional).
- Google and Discord can be linked to an account later.
- Maybe Facebook later; not needed at the start.
- No SimBrief or Navigraph login; it might need an official agreement.

## Flight visibility

Private, link-only or public. See `decisions.md`.

## What's public about a pilot

Name and surname, hours flown, types flown, stats. Profiles are private today; public profiles are planned.

## User-generated content

- Principle: the less user-generated content, the better.
- Some free-text fields and file uploads exist. User content is never republished.
- Logs are kept so we can help the authorities in case of illegal content.

## Deletion

- Self-serve account deletion.
- We keep only a hash of the deleted email, so that address can't sign up again (anti-abuse). This applies to everyone.
- A returning user can ask on Discord and gets unblocked without hesitation.

## Bans

By email hash and by IP. IP bans are expected to be rare.

## Emails

- Mandatory transactional: bans, password recovery and similar.
- Flight-progress updates: e.g. "you haven't finished your flight, something may be off".
- No newsletter yet. Preference switches and opt-ins exist.

### Terms of service in brief

- **Sim-only.** The terms say MyPreflight can't be used for professional or general aviation, real navigation or real tracking. It isn't a flight-planning, weather, dispatch or aeronautical-information service, and nobody may rely on it for an operational decision.
- **Age:** 13 or older. Users aged 13 to 15 need a parent's or guardian's consent where the law requires it.
- **Accounts:** accurate information; email and password, or Google or Discord. The user keeps their credentials safe and reports suspected misuse.
- **Acceptable use:** nothing unlawful or harmful, no unauthorised access, no getting round rate limits or security, no scraping or overloading without written permission, no impersonation, nothing that could endanger people or aircraft.
- **Content:** users keep ownership of what they submit and give MyPreflight a licence to host and process it only to run the service.
- **Price and availability:** free for now, provided "as available", without warranties. Features may change or go away.
- **Liability:** capped, where law allows, at the greater of EUR 50 or what the user paid in the previous 12 months.
- **Ending:** users can stop at any time and request deletion from their settings. MyPreflight may suspend or terminate accounts to protect the service or comply with law.
- **Law:** Polish, without removing consumers' mandatory rights at home.

### Privacy policy in brief

- **Data used:**
  - name, surname, display name or nickname, email address, age confirmation;
  - credentials (password hashed);
  - messages to support and free text entered in the app;
  - IP address, browser and device details, session identifiers, cookies and preferences, usage events, security and audit logs.
- **Why:** to run and secure the account and its features, answer support, prevent abuse, diagnose faults and meet legal duties. Each purpose has its GDPR legal basis listed on the page.
- **Email:** no promotional or marketing email. Operational messages only: account verification, security notices, password resets, service notifications and support replies.
- **Providers named:** DigitalOcean (hosting), Google Sign-In, Discord login, Google reCAPTCHA, Leaflet and OpenStreetMap. Some process data outside the EEA under recognised safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.
- **Cookies:** necessary and preference cookies only, plus Google's when the user signs in with Google or meets a protected form. No advertising or analytics trackers.
- **Retention:**

  | Data | Kept |
  | --- | --- |
  | Account data, user content | Until the account is deleted, unless law or a legal claim requires longer |
  | Support messages | Until no longer needed |
  | Server and security logs | Up to 1 month |
  | Backups | Up to 1 month, then rotated out |
  | Transactional email records | As long as needed for delivery, security or claims |

- **Rights:** access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability and objection under GDPR, and a complaint to the Polish data-protection authority (UODO). No decisions are made solely by automated processing.
- **Children:** not for under-13s; 13 to 15 only with a parent's or guardian's consent.
