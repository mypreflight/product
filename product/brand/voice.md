# Voice

How MyPreflight sounds in the app, on Discord and on social media.

Last reviewed: 2026-10-20

## Rules

- **Calm and professional, like a good airline dispatcher.** A monitoring alert reads "Hey, what's going on?", not "⚠️ ERROR".
- **Plain English and short sentences.** Aviation terms used correctly.
- **Terse, exact and unit-aware in the product.** Tonnes, UTC times written with a `Z` (`06:20Z`), IATA and ICAO codes. Never chatty or gamified.
- **A little flight-geek humour is welcome**, such as a connector called Transponder. Memes and hype are not.
- **Never AI slop.** If a post could be about any product, rewrite it.
- **Specific beats generic.** "3 tonnes of salmon from Keflavik, chilled all the way to New York" beats "realistic cargo".
- **Sim-only.** Never imply real-world operational use.

## House style

- The name is **MyPreflight** in prose. The lowercase **mypreflight** is the logotype only.
- Website rule: no em dashes in user-facing copy. Readers take them as a sign of machine-written text; use a colon, a comma or a full stop.
- Headings on the website are claims in full sentences, not feature nouns.
- English only.
