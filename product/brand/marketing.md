# Marketing

Channels, cadence, post types and release comms.

Last reviewed: 2026-10-20

## Starting point

Oskar has no social media or app marketing experience and wants a researched, documented approach. Claude leads marketing decisions by asking him direct product and marketing questions.

## Before any post

1. Check the feature is **Live** in `../../features/catalogue.md`. Never advertise Partial, Planned or brief-only features.
2. Follow `voice.md`.
3. Use real screenshots, set up as in `screenshots.md`.
4. Oskar reviews every post before it goes out.

## Channels

- **Organic first, no or low budget:** Facebook groups, MSFS forums, flight-sim and add-on Discords (VATSIM included). Subtle peeks, such as sharing a real flight page, not ads.
- **Official profiles:** Instagram, Facebook, probably TikTok. Stories are automated by agents and must be high-quality and engaging.
- **Later:** YouTuber outreach, not on day one. Twitch and YouTube are new territory.
- **Paid ads** only once subscriptions can fund them.

## Cadence

One quality post per day. Quality is the bar; there's time every day to review.

## Post types

- Real long-flight showcases linking to the tracked flight page, saying what was carried (e.g. salmon from Keflavik)
- Flight, connection or airport of the day
- Fun facts
- Feature spotlights
- Themed events and their results (e.g. Munich Day)
- OFP explainers: how to read your OFP, focused on the first 2 or 3 pages
- Postcards
- Community stats once there's traffic, manual first (e.g. "20 departures from CDG this week"), automated later

## Real flights to showcase

Oskar's own flying is a good source of real showcase flights:

- **Types:** A320, A350, A380, 737 MAX 8, 777.
- **Long-haul transatlantic:** e.g. London Heathrow to New York JFK, flown in real time without sim-rate.
- **Short and medium hops, often on VATSIM:** London–Rome, Frankfurt–Copenhagen, Zurich–Larnaca, and city hops to London, Frankfurt, Zurich, Paris, Barcelona and Madrid.

## Review

Oskar reviews every post and refines the agent instructions until the text doesn't sound robotic.

## Release comms

- **Discord #releases:** every feature announced with screenshots, sometimes a full showcase. A redesign gets one before and after graphic per change, each with a one- or two-sentence caption; see `screenshots.md`.
- **In-app "What's new":** planned; not built yet.
- **Public changelog:** planned. Until then, every version gets release notes on GitHub.
- **Screens to capture per feature:** see `screenshots.md`.

## Why automate

Oskar does announcements, screenshots and support alone. Automation frees his time for real community engagement: he's curious where pilots fly and what they carry, and wants the community to see that someone genuinely cares.
