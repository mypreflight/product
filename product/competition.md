# Competition

Who else is in the space and how MyPreflight relates to them.

Last reviewed: 2026-10-20

| Product | Relationship | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Volanta | Main reference | Our difference: data aggregation and real-world context around the flight |
| EFBX | Competitor | Run by a pilot, gets attention, looks solid; likely offers much less context |
| SimBrief | Complementary | They make the numbers with great calculation logic; we add context on top |
| Navigraph | Parallel | Charts and nav data. Navigraph lets only aircraft add-on developers display its charts, so we won't show them without a partnership, and we won't build our own charts viewer. If Navigraph ever added tracking and briefing, the overlap would be large. |
| VA platforms (phpVMS, vAMSYS) | Possible future partners | Two-way integration (e.g. combining VA PIREPs with MyPreflight) so pilots don't run two apps; far future |

Little real competition offers this depth today.
